How scoring works

Scores are calculated only from verified numeric search volume and keyword difficulty. Volume contributes up to 50 points; lower difficulty contributes up to 50. If only one metric is available, the score is normalized against that metric alone. No verified numeric data means no score.

Verification requires a research source and date, or a previously saved verified record.
